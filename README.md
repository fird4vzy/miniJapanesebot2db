# 🇯🇵 Japanese-Russian-English Learning Bot

A comprehensive Telegram bot designed to help users learn Japanese through daily notifications, random word discovery, and an interactive scoring quiz.



## ✨ Features

* **150+ Word Dictionary:** A curated list of JLPT N5-N4 words with translations in English and Russian.
* **Active Learning Quiz:** Multiple-choice questions to test your knowledge.
* **Scoring System:** Earn 10 points for every correct answer and track your progress.
* **Daily Notifications:** Automated daily words sent at 9:00 AM to keep your streak alive.
* **Trilingual Support:** Every word includes Japanese (Kanji/Kana), Romaji, English, and Russian meanings.
